Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Royale Karmen LTD ("Royale Karmen", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with Royale Karmen Publishing and our social media publishing integrations.

1. About Us

Royale Karmen LTD is a United Kingdom company operating the Royale Karmen brand.

2. Purpose of Royale Karmen Publishing

Royale Karmen Publishing is an internal content management and publishing integration used by Royale Karmen LTD to prepare, manage and publish content to social media accounts operated by Royale Karmen LTD.

This may include integrations with platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and other social media services.

3. Information We May Process

When Royale Karmen Publishing connects to a social media platform, we may process information made available through that platform's authorised APIs, including:

  • social media account identifiers;
  • profile and account information;
  • content created or published by Royale Karmen LTD;
  • media files selected for publication;
  • publication status and identifiers;
  • content performance and analytics data, where authorised;
  • technical information necessary to operate the integration.

4. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • authenticate authorised Royale Karmen social media accounts;
  • prepare and publish social media content;
  • manage Royale Karmen content;
  • monitor publication status;
  • analyse content performance where permitted;
  • maintain and improve our internal publishing workflows.

5. TikTok Integration

Royale Karmen Publishing may use TikTok APIs to connect authorised TikTok accounts and publish content on behalf of Royale Karmen LTD.

Information obtained through TikTok APIs is used only for operating the authorised integration and related publishing functionality.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information obtained through our social media integrations.

Information may be processed by service providers and technology platforms that are necessary to operate our publishing infrastructure, subject to their applicable terms and privacy requirements.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, to operate our publishing systems, or to comply with legal obligations.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect credentials, access tokens and information processed by our systems.

9. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights regarding their personal information, including rights of access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing.

10. Third-Party Platforms

Social media platforms operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Use of TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or other platforms is also subject to the policies of those respective services.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, integrations or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact
